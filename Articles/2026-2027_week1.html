Boston Celtics vs Detroit Pistons

Paul George (38 points, 7 three-pointers, 5 rebounds, 3 assists, 5 steals) look absolutely dominant in his first celtics start. However, don't expect him to maintain this level of performance throughout the season. We've seen this before from him in the past.

Jayson Tatum took on more ball-handling responsibilities and ended up with a game high 11 assists. His outside shot was not falling at all.

Jalen Duren was a beast in the paint. They were double boxing him out all game long. If it wasn't for that he may have gotten more offensive rebounds.

Oklahoma City Thunder vs San Antonio Spurs

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander hasn't missed a beat, scoring 32 points on 13-of-27 shooting from the field. New bump foul rules didn't bother him at all as he was making everything. Expect him to be scoring at the same caliber this season.

Victor Wembanyama only made 1 field goal (1-of-13). Still looked like a dominant paint defender as he was looking for blocks all night long. Ended up with 6 blocks mostly on driving wings (Shai, Ajay, JDub).

Stephon Castle. The Thunder let him shoot from outside as much as he wanted, and he sank 5-12 threes.
